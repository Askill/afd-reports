About these reports

These pages are built from the hexane AfD leak: 421,178 emails across 10 mailboxes, plus documents, databases and media. Each report reconstructs one slice of the corpus and publishes the method, the unit of analysis and the provenance alongside the figures.

Reports are rendered offline and shipped as single files. They contain no trackers, no external fonts and no third-party requests.

Addresses are reproduced as they appear in the leaked material. No pseudonymisation is applied in these published reports.
