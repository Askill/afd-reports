## Exposure assessment

Most addresses are institutional or office addresses of public figures, party
organizations, parliamentary offices and government ministries. A minority are
private mailboxes (web.de, gmx.de, freenet.de, mailbox.org) and business
addresses. No phone numbers, postal addresses, dates of birth, IBANs or
credentials are published.

**Risk:** low for public figures, party offices and government offices; moderate
for private individuals whose personal mailboxes appear. Published as-is per
explicit user approval on 2026-10-23.
